# Social platform logos

Official assets downloaded on 2026-09-16:

- `x.svg`: `logo.svg` from the [X brand toolkit](https://about.x.com/en/who-we-are/brand-toolkit), `x-logo.zip`. Original white vector artwork, displayed in black on light backgrounds using CSS inversion.
- `instagram.png`: `01 Static Glyph/01 Gradient Glyph/Instagram_Glyph_Gradient.png` from the [Instagram brand resources](https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/instagram/instagram-brand/), `IG_brand_asset_pack_2023.zip`.
- `facebook.png`: `Logo/Primary Logo/Facebook_Logo_Primary.png` from the [Facebook brand resources](https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/facebook/logo/), `Facebook-Brand-Asset-Pack.zip`.

PNG assets are proportionally reduced to 128 × 128 pixels for UI use. Shapes and colors are unchanged. These marks belong to their respective owners and are subject to their brand guidelines.
